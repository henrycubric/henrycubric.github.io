About Henry

Builder.
Thinker. Doer.

I’m Henry Cubric — a Vancouver-based student entrepreneur who builds things that actually matter. At 16, I founded On the Edge Engineering, a design and prototyping studio where engineering precision meets creative ambition.

My work sits at the intersection of CAD design, 3D printing, and product development — turning concepts into tangible, real-world objects. I believe the best ideas don’t live on slides; they live in your hands.

Beyond the workshop, I mentor younger makers, compete in DECA and SHAD, and show up for my community in ways that go beyond the machine.

The Studio

On the Edge
Engineering

On the Edge Engineering is a Vancouver-based design and prototyping studio built on one premise: that great products come from the relentless refinement of an idea until it’s exactly right.

From concept to CAD to finished product — every project is handled with precision, creativity, and a genuine commitment to the craft. We don’t just print things. We engineer them.
