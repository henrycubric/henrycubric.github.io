How to play

Seven prompts a day, the same for everyone, 25 seconds each. Run out the clock with nothing typed and the planet is lost. Every answer becomes a planet, and how many players gave the same answer decides how far from Goldie it lands. Play today's star, then climb the galaxy map: short levels, up to three stars each, and boosters you earn along the way. Answers must be on the list; small typos get autocorrected, and anything else asks you to try again while the clock keeps running.

Too hot

8% or more said it. Too obvious: it lands close and scorches into a lava rock.

Too cold

Under 2% said it. Too obscure: it drifts out and freezes into an ice ball.

Just right

2 to 8% said it. It lands in the Goldilocks zone and grows life. Hit the gold ring at the center (about 4%) for a bullseye worth double.

Call your shot. Drag the aim marker to where you think your answer will land. The closer you call it, the bigger your multiplier, and a perfect call earns rings or a moon. Habitable planets in a row evolve: plants, creatures, then tiny cities.
